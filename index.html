How It Works

One simple system. Two jobs.

Your inquiries shouldn't have to depend on you remembering where every conversation started.

01 — Answer

FAQ & Inquiry Bot

For your website or LinkedIn

Give potential clients immediate answers to the questions you get all the time — your services, pricing, coaching process, availability, who you work with, and more.

The bot uses information you provide, so potential clients can get answers without waiting for a response to every basic question. If someone wants to learn more, the inquiry flow captures their information for you.

Answer the repetitive questions without answering the same message over and over.

02 — Capture & See

Lead Dashboard

Your simple inquiry view

When someone expresses interest, their information is captured and organized in one simple dashboard. You can see who's interested and where each inquiry stands.

  • Name and contact information
  • What they're interested in
  • Date of inquiry
  • Simple status tracking: New → Reviewed → Contacted → Booked
  • Notes and next step

No digging through DMs, emails, forms, and random notes trying to remember who said what.
